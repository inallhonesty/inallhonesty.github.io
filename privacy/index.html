This policy explains what information the game uses. We want it short and honest.

What we collect

Only if you agree: anonymous usage statistics. The game asks once whether you want to share them, and you can change your answer any time in Options → Share anonymous statistics. If you say no, nothing is sent.

When you agree, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google Ireland Limited) to record:

  • game events: a patrol started (which captain), a battle's round and result, how a patrol ended, the tutorial finished or skipped, and which Captain's Orders item was picked;
  • automatic app information: first launch, sessions and time played, the app version, the device model and operating system version, language and approximate country (from the network, not GPS);
  • an app-instance ID: a random identifier for this installation, not linked to your name, email or Google account.

We never collect your name, email, contacts or precise location, the game has no accounts, and statistics never use your advertising ID. We use the statistics only to understand how the game is played and to improve it, for example its balance and difficulty. We don't sell them or use them for advertising.

Who processes the data: Google, as our processor, under its Firebase terms. Google may process it outside the EU under the EU's standard contractual clauses.

  • Retention: statistics are kept for 14 months, then deleted.
  • Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Options; withdrawing stops all collection from then on.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: your profile (currencies, levels, unlocks, quests), your settings and a patrol in progress. This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

The game shows only ads you choose to watch for a bonus (for example double Supplies or a quest refresh). Ads are never forced, and a player who never watches one still gets every reward that isn't an ad bonus.

Ads come from Google AdMob (provided by Google Ireland Limited). When ads may be shown, Google receives:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information: model, operating system, app version and language;
  • your IP address, from which it estimates an approximate location;
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether you watched or tapped them.

Your choice: where the law requires it (for example in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks the first time you start the game whether ads may be personalized. If you say no, you still see ads, but they're based only on context, not on your interests. Change your answer any time in Options → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads).

Google uses this information under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. We don't receive your advertising ID or any personal information from the ads.

  • Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, collected by Google's consent form where the law requires it; you can withdraw it any time in Options → Ad privacy choices.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

You can turn statistics off at any time in Options. Under the GDPR you may also ask us for access to or deletion of statistics linked to your app-instance ID, by writing to the address below. Because the statistics are anonymous to us, include the date you installed the game so we can help. You can delete everything stored on your phone by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP).

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes what it does with information, and change the date above.
