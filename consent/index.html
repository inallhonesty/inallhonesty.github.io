Consent settings

Anonymous statistics: you choose. The game asks once, and you can switch it on or off any time in Options → Share anonymous statistics. When it's on, the game sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase; the Privacy Policy lists exactly what. When it's off, nothing is sent.

Ads: the game only shows ads you choose to watch for a bonus; they're never forced. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), Google's consent form asks the first time you start the game whether ads may be personalised. Change your answer any time in Options → Ad privacy choices (the button appears where that choice applies). Statistics never use your advertising ID.
