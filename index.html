Sonar Lock

A Cold War submarine auto-battler for Android.
Pack your hull, find the enemy first, and outlast rival subs in silent duels.

Coming soon to Google Play

The game

  • Fit torpedoes, sonar, engines, armor and crew into your submarine like a puzzle.
  • Every battle is a detection contest: go loud and hunt, or go quiet and strike first.
  • Six captains, refits, fires, flooding and mutiny, and patrols short enough for a bus ride.
  • No pay-to-win. Ads only when you choose to watch one for a bonus.
