Delete your Sonar Lock data

Sonar Lock is made by InAllHonesty (SC DREAMWEAVER SRL). You can delete your online data and account at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Options.
  2. Tap Delete my online data, then confirm.

It deletes straight away, from our servers: your shared submarine builds, your cloud save (with its backups and purchase records), your ranked records (rank, points, rating, ranked patrols and submarines, battle records, season rewards and your place on the leaderboard) and your online account. Your progress on your phone stays. If a server can't be reached, the game says so and you can try again; nothing is skipped. Nothing goes back up to our servers until you start a patrol again.

By email

If you can't open the game (for example, you uninstalled it), write to sonarlock.game@gmail.com from any address. To help us find your data, include your player ID if you have it (Options → Your player ID) or the Google Play Games profile you play with. We delete it within 30 days and write back when it's done.

What we keep

To stop deletion being used to cheat Ranked, the ranked server keeps a few scrambled records that hold no account ID and no name: a scrambled form of each purchase code it checked, and, only while an account is blocked for suspicious activity, a scrambled marker of it. They are deleted 2 years after the account's last ranked activity. The Privacy Policy (section 12) explains this in full.
